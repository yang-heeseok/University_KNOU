## (B-3) Barney(1991) 원전 용어 교차참조

### Takeaway
Barney(1991)는 imperfect imitability의 원천을 (a) unique historical conditions(경로의존), (b) causal ambiguity, (c) social complexity 세 가지로 제시; '특허'는 Barney & Hesterly VRIO 교재에서 'patents'로 추가 논의됨.

### Cited Findings
- Barney(1991) 3요인: historical conditions(path dependence), causal ambiguity, social complexity; 인과적 모호성 정의 "link between resources and SCA cannot be understood, or understood very imperfectly" — [Redalyc 논문(2차 인용)](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/1551/155142631007.pdf); [Management Science 2009](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/references/10.1287/mnsc.1080.0938)
- VRIO 결과 4단계: competitive disadvantage / competitive parity / temporary competitive advantage / sustained competitive advantage (V+R = temporary; V+R+I+O = sustained) — [MindTools VRIO](https://www.mindtools.com/?p=65158)

### Inferences
- 과제의 '법적 요인(특허)'은 Barney & Hesterly(Strategic Management and Competitive Advantage) 'patents' 논의를 근거로 쓰는 것이 안전; Barney(1991) 원전에는 3요인만.

### Gaps
- Barney(1991) JoM 원문 및 Barney & Hesterly 교재 원문 직접 조회 안 함(2차 요약 의존).
